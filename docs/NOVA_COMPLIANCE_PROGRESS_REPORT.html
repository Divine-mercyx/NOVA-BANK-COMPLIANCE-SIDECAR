1. Executive summary

Nova Bank identified two strategic gaps: (1) automation of NFIU reportable transactions (CTR, FTR, PEP, etc.) from Finacle into a staging platform, and (2) online real-time screening of all local and foreign currency transactions. The Compliance Sidecar addresses these requirements through a dedicated portal and machine-to-machine Export API, without modifying Finacle core.

3. Milestone 1 — What has been delivered

3.1 Data pipeline

  • Extract from Finacle Oracle (TBAADM.HTD joined to TBAADM.GAM) or from UAT CSV export when Oracle over VPN is slow.
  • Validate accounts, amounts, and names; map channels (NIP, RTGS, SWIFT, and others present in HTD).
  • Stage in PostgreSQL with CTR, FTR, PEP, and STR flags and internal nfiu_payload (translated JSON per transaction).
  • Background ETL, date-range extraction, duplicate skip, Oracle thick-mode client for bank password compatibility.

3.2 Compliance portal (frontend)

  • Dashboard, Extraction (date picker), Data Quality, Reports (CTR/FTR/PEP/STR workflow), Audit Trail, Team & roles.
  • Integration: API Keys (admin) and API Docs (live testing with copy response / cURL).

3.4 NFIU reporting formats — gap and ask

Required from Nova Compliance: Official NFIU goAML XSD and field specifications for CTR, FTR, PEP, and STR. Current outputs are structurally consistent and API-ready but are not yet certified as filing-identical to NFIU portal requirements. Once specifications and sandbox access are provided, report generation and Export API payloads will be aligned field-for-field.

3.5 Finacle / Oracle performance

Direct HTD reads over VPN can be slow on large date windows. Mitigations in software: narrow date ranges, day-by-day chunked extract, CSV UAT path. Recommendation to DBA/IT: read-only grants, confirm listener port (1625 vs 1521), date indexes/statistics, or replica/scheduled export for production volumes.

4. Milestone 2 — Transaction screening

4.1 What screening means in this project

Before or during a payment, customer and counterparty names are checked against sanctions, PEP, and internal watchlists. A match does not always mean a block—it creates a compliance alert for analysts to review (false positive vs true hit) and documents the decision for audit.

4.2 What has been built (delivered in the sidecar)

Delivered — Operations

  • Alert Queue — unified inbox for pending screening hits; search and detail view.
  • Analyst workflow — Approve (e.g. false positive), Reject, Escalate to senior approver; role-based access.
  • Screening Log & Performance — history of decisions, latency and volume metrics for management reporting.
  • Simulate flags — training/demo mode generating sample alerts without live external lists (for process UAT).

Delivered — Technical integration

  • Synchronous screening check — middleware can POST a transaction; sidecar returns clear or review and optionally creates a PENDING alert in the queue.
  • PEP sample registry and keyword-based demo matching for proof-of-concept.
  • Audit events for screening actions (aligned with Alert Queue decisions).

6. Conclusion

The Compliance Sidecar materially advances Nova’s first objective—automating capture and reporting of Finacle transactions into a staging platform with IT-accessible translated exports. Development is approximately 68–72% complete prior to formal UAT, with Milestone 1 near 80% and Milestone 2 screening workflow at approximately 40%, pending vendor-led live list integration. Provision of NFIU official formats and screening vendor selection are the two critical path items to move from successful technical demo to production-ready compliance operations.
